PeriScope pig external perturbation-response validation, 2026-10-03.
Scope: user-authorized completion of independent external blood-input/brain-response evaluation. Supplemental evidence only. No changes to A6/A7 or candidate eligibility. No ESM2, no new target gate, no manuscript/main-figure edits.

Known evidence before this protocol: ten animals (six saline/four systemic LPS); published bulk blood and prefrontal brain transcript TPM; published sampling durations; Ensembl-only mapping coverage and nonzero counts; author publication describes inflammatory brain responses. This is a prospectively locked computational evaluation within an already known public study, not blinded discovery of an unknown intervention.

Data: Ensembl116 one-to-one mapping, all3730 mapped model features from provided transcript TPM sums. CPM is retained in the mapping delivery, not mixed into TPM. Missing features are held at the human reference expression in response transfer; they are not assigned measured zero. 3560 high-confidence orthologs form a reported evaluation sensitivity, not a nomination filter. Original zeros remain original zeros. Current frozen seed42 checkpoint (4741genes,228ADT). Ten already-trained seeds42–51 provide sensitivity without retraining or choosing a favorable seed.

Input adapter: control-referenced response transfer onto human training blood cells, not a claim of measured pig cell-type profiles. Within each sample, common-gene abundance is scaled to10000. Compute log((sample+pseudocount)/(mean-control+pseudocount)), clip symmetrically, multiply by a shrinkage gain, then multiply actual human reference-cell CP10k by exp(delta). Preserve nonmapped genes and the implicit nonmodel library remainder; renormalize total cell library to10000. Human cells are actual control training cells sampled equally across available donors (128/source state). Same reference cells and posterior random draws are used for all pigs/baselines. No synthetic cell is treated as a biological replicate.

Adapter selection: human training donor leave-one-out transfer from all-blood-cell pseudobulk to each of the three source-state pseudobulks; same-cohort training control donors form the reference. Pseudocount {0.1,1,10}, gain {0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1}, log-fold cap {ln2,ln4,ln8}. Select minimum donor/state-balanced log1p expression-change MSE on mapped features. Tie favors lower gain. Independent existing human validation donors are assessed once after selection, without retuning. Zero gain is an allowed honest outcome; unshrunk gain1 is a prespecified sensitivity if selected gain is0, not a replacement claimed as validated. Human whole-blood granulocyte truth is not available; the adapter check uses the project's PBMC reference and its limitation is retained.

Frozen prediction: source states cDC/classical_mono/nonclassical_mono; receivers astro/microglia_mhc2, matching the six previously benchmarked supported axes. Primary disease condition is held at human control for EVERY pig, so LPS labels cannot drive predictions. Fixed human PD condition is a prespecified sensitivity. No pig brain values are available to adapter selection or predictor code. Target is model-predicted glial response compared with measured bulk brain response; it is not claimed to reconstruct measured pig single-cell subtype expression. Source and receiver axes reported in full; equal-axis aggregate is primary, no best-axis selection.

Comparators: frozen human-trained Gaussian affine OT, direct common-PCA OT-paired Ridge, official CellOT common-PCA model; no-blood zero-response reference; direct blood-response identity reference (tests shared systemic expression alone). Existing baselines reuse identical input adapter. OT training pairs are computational, not real blood–brain pairs. For PeriScope and latent-input Ridge, existing frozen encoder/protein head are used. ADTs are inferred; no pig ADT measurement is claimed.

Scale/readout: model decoder outputs are centered log1p(CP10k). Restore human brain center, floor negative implied log expression to0, transform to linear CP10k, average across reference cells, and score each animal's log2 ratio to the six predicted control animals (0.1CP10k pseudocount). Observed pig brain uses common-gene-scaled provided TPM and the same0.1 pseudocount, separately normalized to its own controls. Thus the endpoint is relative response, not absolute cross-species abundance. Mean LPS minus mean control log response across genes is the group effect. Equal-axis aggregation is on predicted response; individual axes remain visible.

Primary evaluation: Spearman correlation between predicted and measured LPS-control brain response across all mapped genes. Report Pearson, sign agreement, response RMSE, magnitude ratio, and improvement vs zero response; correlation alone cannot establish calibrated magnitude. No gene-wise p-values treating genes as independent replicates. Exact210 brain-label assignments evaluate group-direction correlation conditional on the locked prediction. Paired donor bootstrap1000 draws resamples matched blood–brain animals within group and recomputes contrasts. No brain-derived calibration of prediction amplitude or sign.

Individual pairing: remove group means separately from predictions and observations; compare matched cross-gene cosine similarity to all6!*4!=17280 within-group reassigned pairings. This tests additional individual information beyond treatment groups. Report distinct from group contrast. Zero-response baselines have undefined correlation, not a fabricated zero correlation. All statistical outputs retained, with BH over the four nonconstant aggregate model primary correlation tests; other arms/conditions/seeds/subsets are labelled sensitivity/exploratory.

Additional readouts: top100 receiver-state marker genes per receiver, selected solely from human brain training donor-balanced mean linear expression vs neural_other (pseudocount0.1), positive enrichment and meanCP10k>0.1; marker subset plus high-confidence ortholog subset reported without choosing by pig response. Animal leave-one-out sensitivity records the sampling-time imbalance (190–562min LPS,300–310min controls). No time-adjusted causal claim is made from10animals.

Freeze sequence: protocol and source hashes -> human adapter selection/validation + reference sampling -> all external predictions persisted and hashed -> open pig brain expression for evaluation -> figures/report. No retraining or favorable hyperparameter tuning after external outcome evaluation. Implementation errors may be corrected with documented reasons, not silent endpoint changes.
