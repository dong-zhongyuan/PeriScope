Current-pipeline benchmark — frozen before new result evaluation, 2026-10-02
Scope: answer whether blood information and nonlinear transport improve current brain predictions; primary axes cDC->astro and nonclassical_mono->astro (the two manuscript targets). Broader states are contextual, not post-hoc substitutes for these axes.
Inputs: current ccwm_v23 1469-gene tensors; original 9 training / 9 test brain donors, blood cohort unchanged; no artificial blood-brain cell pairing. This test split has previously been inspected; this is a benchmark on the existing split, not a newly independent validation.
Seeds: 42,43,44. Same source draws and same target donor draws for every arm. Biological inference based on donor, not cell counts or seeds.
Arms: (1) disease/state-only donor-balanced mean, (2) disease/state-only donor-balanced resampling (strong no-blood distribution comparator), (3) additive mean-shift mapping from blood, (4) Gaussian affine optimal transport in shared 64-dimensional PCA, (5) upstream CellOT ICNN transport in that same PCA, (6) frozen PeriScope checkpoint.
PCA fit only on source blood and brain training donors, equal contributions from each condition and tissue. No test cells in PCA or fitting. Gaussian OT and CellOT use identical PCA and training samples. Gene-space reconstructions and shared-PCA metrics both reported to quantify dimensionality effects.
Two evaluation settings kept distinct: conditional distribution prediction (actual disease label allowed); fixed-control mapping (same control-conditioned mapping applied to PD and control blood). A label-only model has zero fixed-condition differential by construction; its train-brain PD-control contrast is also reported as a separate disease-only comparator, so zero is not treated as a competitive victory.
PeriScope primary readout retains published zero-protein input; inferred-protein readout is a prespecified diagnostic because training Block A uses inferred proteins. Do not silently replace manuscript results.
Training donor weighting and prediction donor weighting are equal within disease group. Report compatibility sensitivity with the original cell-pooled evaluation where needed.
Metrics: donor mean-profile MSE, energy distance per gene, Gaussian MMD using train-defined bandwidth, variance ratio; signed disease-difference Spearman and fixed-program contrast error. Legacy pairwise-normalized Sinkhorn retained only as a secondary diagnostic because independently normalizing each OT term obscures physical scale.
CellOT uses official archived source (bunnech/cellot commit recorded in existing repo history), ICNN/losses copied verbatim; shared PCA is an explicit adaptation. Adam 1e-4, (0.5,0.9), batch256, 10 g steps per f step. Maximum100000 outer iterations; evaluate fixed training-only reference MMD every2000, select best, stop after five non-improving checks after >=10000; refit is unnecessary because selection reference is training-only. It is not an independent validation set. Record learning curve, budget and convergence status. No tuning on brain test outcomes. A short smoke run checks execution only and is excluded from results.
Results saved regardless of direction; no target/seed/gene-set/ROI selection based on improved P values. Target-specific statistical audits and external perturbation feasibility are recorded separately. Original manuscript and figures remain unchanged until new results are adjudicated.
